## About Me

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where he conducts research at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.hkust.edu.cn/) under the supervision of [Professor Junxian He](https://nlp.hkust.edu.cn/~jhe/). He graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn) in June 2024 with a B.Eng.

His research focuses on **Natural Language Processing** and **Machine Learning**. His research interests include:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
