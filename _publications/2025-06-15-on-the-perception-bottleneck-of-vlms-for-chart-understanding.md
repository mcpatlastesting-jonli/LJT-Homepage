This pre-print paper investigates the **perception bottleneck** of vision-language models (VLMs) when applied to chart understanding tasks, analyzing the limitations in how VLMs process visual information from charts. The associated code repository is available on [GitHub (Vision4Chart)](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Vision4Chart).
