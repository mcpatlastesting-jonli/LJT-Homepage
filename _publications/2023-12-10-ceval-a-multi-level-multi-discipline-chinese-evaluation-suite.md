This paper, presented at [NeurIPS 2023](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2023), introduces **C-Eval**, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models that spans multiple levels and disciplines, providing a standardized benchmark for assessing Chinese language understanding and reasoning capabilities.
